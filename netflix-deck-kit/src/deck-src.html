Culture, in two parts.

China Culture

Company Culture

China Culture, what I’ve noticed.

Languages

Mandarin across China. Cantonese in Guangdong, Hong Kong and Macau.

Warm water

Warm or hot water, rarely cold.

Midday nap

A short nap after lunch is normal at work.

No number 4

四 sounds like 死 (death). OnePlus 3T → 5, Oppo Find X3 → X5, vivo X30 → X50.

One time zone

A country wide enough for 5 time zones runs on one: Beijing Time (UTC+8).

English names

Many use an English name with international colleagues, in China and abroad. 李明 → Leo.

Cashless

Almost everything is paid by QR code with WeChat Pay or Alipay.

Local apps

Google is blocked in mainland China. Locals use Baidu, Amap, DiDi and Bilibili.
